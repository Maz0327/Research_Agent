## The two structural changes both agents converged on
**A. Fix the primitive, not the call sites.** One weak instrument —
bag-of-content-words containment with boundary-less substring matching — sits
under the grounding gate, coverage gate, quote verification, dispute routing,
dispute evidence, harvest dedup and the recall audit. Findings 3-6 are one root
cause. Harden it once (word boundaries, negation preserved with a polarity veto,
one-passage co-occurrence, ordered shingles for quotes) and every consumer
inherits it.

**B. One registry of the document surface.** briefing_prose, lint's
_sections_of, strip, the citations validator, both renderers and the repair
passes each hand-maintain their own list of what the document contains. Every
list is one section behind the newest section — that is the mojibake, the
numbering and the organisations class of bug. A single declarative registry
(field path, written-by model/code/harvest, editable-by-repair, cited) that all
of them iterate makes "model-written but never gated" structurally impossible.

**Writing side adds:** make the RA's canonical JSON the episode's single truth
surface, copied into the episode at 4b, with renders, the stage-4 worksheet, the
ballot and the delta-scan all derived from it by code. Kills 16, 17 and half of
20.
